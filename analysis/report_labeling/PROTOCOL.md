**Protocol recorded before development-report inspection and extractor evaluation**

Study-level validation of an interpretable report-to-condition extractor against 58 organizer-labeled MRI studies. No MRI training, image predictions, external report transmission, source changes, or extraction on the other 4,349 studies.

Use 40 development and 18 held-out studies, seed 20260913. Select a prevalence-balanced subset using gold labels only; this use of labels for stratification is not extraction evaluation. Keep normalized duplicate reports in one partition (the current 58 are all distinct). Patient-level independence is not established by a StudyInstanceUID split. Do not change the split after seeing extraction results. Previous MRI pilot performance is separate and cannot validate report extraction.

Inspect development reports and targets while constructing terminology. Do not inspect held-out report text or extraction outcomes before freezing code, configuration and evaluation logic. Then evaluate the held-out set once; final disagreement analysis is descriptive only. Record hashes in freeze_manifest.json and reject reruns with changed frozen source/configuration. If a bug requires a post-evaluation fix, preserve the original result and declare the holdout used; do not silently replace it.

States: positive, negative, uncertain, not_mentioned. Unknown severity, unclear temporality, anatomy ambiguity, contradictory evidence or uncertain diagnosis must abstain unless the written rules resolve them. Keep evidence, normalized finding, section, severity/history flags, rule ID, method/version and heuristic confidence. Confidence is not a calibrated probability. Nonmention is never converted to negative by the extractor.

Primary metrics: TP/FP/TN/FN and sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, F1, accuracy, balanced accuracy, and Cohen's kappa on the explicitly positive/negative subset; always report its denominator and coverage alongside the full 18-study prevalence and uncertain/nonmentioned counts. Abstentions are not silently counted as correct negatives or removed without disclosure. Also report all-study correct-label yield and positive/negative capture rates including abstentions in the reference denominators. Undefined ratios are missing, never zero. Macro averages exclude undefined values and report how many conditions contribute.

Wilson 95% intervals accompany sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, coverage and nonmention-negative agreement. A stratified holdout of 18 is too small to certify many condition-specific rates. Study counts, not the 216 condition cells, are the independent sample size. Label-yield projections to 4,349 use held-out acceptance rates only, with Wilson ranges; they are extrapolations, not actual extracted or quality-approved labels.

Before seeing results, define an exploratory high-confidence tier as confidence >=0.90 and state positive/negative. A condition is not considered demonstrated safe to scale unless this tier has PPV and NPV Wilson lower bounds >=0.80, at least five predicted positives and five predicted negatives, and >=50% coverage. These are conservative research acceptance gates, not clinical deployment criteria. A condition with unsupported denominators remains unvalidated even with apparent perfect accuracy. Analyze nonmention versus gold after freeze, without altering baseline labels or asserting an organizer omission convention.

Use the organizer clinical definitions at https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rsna-knee-abnormality-detection/discussion/733343. Image-reader borderline-negative guidance does not establish that a missing or uncertain statement in a report is negative. Preserve this distinction.
