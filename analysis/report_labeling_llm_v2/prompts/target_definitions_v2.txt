Draft report operationalization of organizer definitions. This is not a new image-reading standard.
ACL: high-grade partial disruption (more than 50%) or complete anterior cruciate ligament tear; isolated low-grade sprain or degeneration does not meet the target.
MCL: high-grade acute partial or complete medial collateral ligament tear; low-grade sprain and chronic healed injury do not meet the target.
Medial Meniscus: definite medial meniscus tear or pathological morphology; isolated intrasubstance degeneration does not meet the target. An explicit report diagnosis can support a tear; do not invent image-level observations.
Lateral Meniscus: definite lateral meniscus tear or pathological morphology; isolated intrasubstance degeneration does not meet the target. An explicit report diagnosis can support a tear; do not invent image-level observations.
Medial OA: medial tibiofemoral cartilage loss deeper than 50% thickness over a moderate or large area, approximately at least 1 cm in extent. Require compartment and meaningful severity/extent evidence; do not substitute osteophytes alone.
Lateral OA: lateral tibiofemoral cartilage loss deeper than 50% thickness over a moderate or large area, approximately at least 1 cm in extent. Require compartment and meaningful severity/extent evidence; do not substitute osteophytes alone.
PF OA: patellofemoral cartilage loss deeper than 50% thickness over a moderate or large area, approximately at least 1 cm in extent. Require compartment and meaningful severity/extent evidence; do not substitute osteophytes alone.
Effusion: moderate or large joint effusion. Explicit trace/small fluid is below the target threshold; unquantified fluid remains uncertain.
Synovitis: inflamed or thickened synovial lining. Effusion alone does not establish synovitis.
Baker's: moderate or large popliteal Baker cyst. Explicit small cyst is below the target threshold; unspecified size remains uncertain.
Contusion: traumatic bone marrow injury without a fracture line at the same site. Isolated degenerative edema does not establish contusion.
Fracture: an acute cortical break or fracture line. A clearly healed remote fracture is outside this target; unclear timing remains uncertain.
V2 clarification, applied to every target: keep original thresholds; do not infer missing severity or extent. A relevant finding with insufficient qualifying information is uncertain. Explicit below-threshold findings can be negative; absent discussion is not_mentioned. A normal meniscus cannot support positive meniscal pathology. Report contradictions or unresolved current/history/postoperative ambiguity require uncertain rather than selecting whichever statement is convenient. Qualifiers about one anatomical structure must not be transferred to another.
For OA, trochlear or patellar defects concern PF OA, not a tibiofemoral target, regardless of whether their location is described as medial or lateral. A statement about one normal surface does not exclude pathology elsewhere in that compartment. Severe cartilage loss without sufficient compartment or extent information cannot be automatically positive.
For ACL/MCL, an unspecified interstitial or partial tear does not establish the required high-grade disruption. Do not infer a percentage from the presence of the word tear.
For Baker's, a popliteal vein abnormality is not cyst evidence. For Synovitis, fluid alone is not evidence for a positive or negative lining finding. For Contusion, the target is bone, not muscle; degenerative edema alone is insufficient.
